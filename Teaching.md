I love teaching mathematics and statistics and helping the next generation build confidence in these subjects. I believe every student can develop these skills with encouragement, effective instruction, and opportunities to connect what they learn to real-world applications and their future interests. As artificial intelligence transforms how we learn and work, I aim to help students think critically, adapt to emerging technologies, and use these tools thoughtfully and responsibly.
